About Vassant Finance

Founded in 2023 · Riverside, California

Our Story

We started the Vassant mission back in 2023 with a simple conversation about how, as individual investors, we could best position ourselves to achieve higher, more consistent returns. For many years prior, Blake, one of our co-founders, had discovered a passion for capital markets and channeled that into a hobby, spending significant time researching companies and their fundamentals. Unlike many investors who entered the market during the same time frame, he adopted a long-term mindset and learned to capitalize on time arbitrage, defined as the ability to outwait short-term volatility until stock prices more accurately reflected underlying business performance.

One downside to this process, however, was the significant time and effort required to execute it effectively. From reading 10-Ks and 10-Qs to listening to earnings calls and conducting industry research, both retrieving the right information and distilling it into an investment thesis that built conviction required significant time and effort. This problem was partially mitigated by the rise of Artificial Intelligence. But as AI continues to make investment research more accessible and easier to distill, the challenge is increasingly shifting from accessing the information to organizing, validating, and acting on it. Vassant directly solves this problem by providing a structured framework where investors can consolidate this research alongside their own analysis, transforming scattered information into a documented investment thesis that builds conviction.

The other half of the puzzle, however, is capital allocation. While investing success is often viewed as a stock-picking problem, we believe it is equally a decision-making problem. Identifying a great business is only one half of the battle; determining which accounts to set up, how much capital to allocate, when to deploy additional capital, how a position fits within each portfolio, and maintaining conviction in everything are equally important. Yet these decisions are often made inconsistently, relying on memory, intuition, or scattered notes rather than structured processes. This is where we saw another significant opportunity. By documenting target portfolio allocations, desired purchase prices, investment theses, and the reasoning behind every position, investors can use Vassant to make future decisions with greater consistency and confidence rather than reacting emotionally to short-term market movements.

The Name

Once we started building, we wanted to develop a brand name that uniquely represented how we envisioned our product. We chose the name Vassant. The 'V-' is for creating value and '-assant' is taken from 'En Passant,' a strategic chess move relatively unknown to casual players where a pawn can capture an opponent's pawn that has just advanced two squares on its first move. In this way, users can take advantage of the platform to strategically organize their investment decisions and capital allocation to create more consistent long-term value in their portfolios. It is a core belief of ours that conviction is the key to long-term returns: if you know what you bought and why you bought it, you won't be tempted to sell when the market drops suddenly. History has repeatedly shown that some of the strongest market gains occur shortly after periods of significant declines, making the ability to stay invested one of the most important drivers of long-term returns. This conviction, across everything investing, is precisely what we help build.

Our Team

Our team brings together expertise from software, capital markets solutions, creative design, and even biological research. While our backgrounds are diverse, they share a common foundation of building structured systems to solve complex problems. We approached investing the same way. More importantly, we built Vassant because we experienced these challenges firsthand. We know what it is like to have investment research scattered across different documents and the difficulty of managing multiple portfolios with different strategies. Rather than building another application focused on trading or portfolio performance, we created an investment planning and decision-support platform. Underlying everything we built is the core belief that successful long-term investing is less about making more decisions than making better decisions.
