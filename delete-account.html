Delete Your Account

Vassant Finance LLC — app name: Vassant

You can delete your Vassant account and everything stored in it at any time. There are two ways to do it, and both remove the same data.

1. Delete It Yourself, In the App

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Vassant app and sign in.
  2. Open the side menu and tap Account Information.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  4. Type the phrase delete my account to confirm, and enter your password if your account has one you set. Accounts created through Sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook do not have a password, so those confirm with the phrase alone.
  5. Tap Delete account.

Your account and its data are removed from our production database at that moment. You are signed out, and the account can no longer be used to sign in. This cannot be undone, and support cannot restore it.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you have lost access to your account and cannot sign in, email support@vassantfinance.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will verify the request and delete the account for you.

Requests received this way are honored within 30 days, and in practice are handled within a few business days.

3. What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account removes all of the following from our production database:

  • Your profile — name, email address, and time zone
  • Your sign-in credentials, including password hash, two-factor secret, and session tokens
  • Any linked Apple, Google, or Facebook sign-in records
  • Your financial framework and every allocation in it
  • Your budget, income, and expense entries
  • Your investment accounts, holdings, targets, plans, and purchase history
  • Your vaults, vault pages, and vault templates — including every note you wrote
  • Your connected brokerage links, synced holdings, and auto-sync records
  • Your course progress, quiz results, spotlight profiles, and badges
  • Your notification settings, preferences, delivered notifications, and device push tokens
  • Your referrals, referral credits, and subscription record

4. Connected Bank and Brokerage Data

If you connected an institution through Plaid, deleting your account also revokes that connection with Plaid and instructs Plaid to delete the corresponding data on its side. You can additionally manage or delete data Plaid holds about you directly at my.plaid.com.

5. What Is Retained, and For How Long

A small amount of data outlives the deletion, in every case for a specific reason:

  • Encrypted backup snapshots — deleted data may persist in routine database backups until those snapshots expire, which happens within 7 days. Backups are not used to restore deleted accounts.
  • Billing and tax records — if you ever held a paid subscription, Stripe retains transaction records as its own controller, and we retain the minimum billing history required by tax and accounting law. This does not include your app data.
  • Legal holds — data subject to an active legal hold, fraud investigation, or a specific legal obligation is retained until that obligation ends.

Nothing retained under the above is used to rebuild your account or to contact you.

6. Deleting Data Without Deleting Your Account

If you want to remove specific data but keep your account, you can do that in the app: disconnect an institution to revoke its Plaid connection and remove its synced holdings, delete individual vaults, holdings, or budget entries, or turn off product analytics at Settings > Privacy > Share usage analytics.
